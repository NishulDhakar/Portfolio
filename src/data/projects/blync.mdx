# Blync – Practice Placement Game Rounds

Built as a college project, Blync has grown into a practical platform helping students prepare for game-based placement rounds.

1,000+ signups in just 2 months

Surprising how rewarding it feels to see students actively improving their skills

At first glance, it looks like a simple game site, but under the hood, it leverages:  

- **Backtracking**  
- **2D Matrix logic**  
- **Hashing**  
- **Greedy algorithms**

## How Students Benefit

- **Enhance logic, memory, and analytical skills** for real placement challenges  
- **Monitor progress** with clear performance insights  
- **Compete with peers** and climb the leaderboard  
- **Access guided hints and solutions** to master each game  
- **Gain a head start in placement rounds** with practice on games that are rarely available publicly  

Blync is not just fun—it’s a **strategic tool for placement success**.

## Milestones & Impact

Since launch:  

- **1,000+ signups** in just 2 months  
- **Got 100k** views in 2 months

Recent upgrades:

Next goal: **dynamic, real-time leaderboards** to increase competition
